01 / NOTEBOOK

Things I
think about.

Short pieces of writing, observations and ideas. Unfiltered notes on web design, walking, and useful site building.

08.08.26
The internet is getting boring.
+
Everything is becoming optimized for the same attention span, the same format, the same reaction. Maybe the interesting websites are the ones that don't care about any of that.
02.08.26
Make things before you need them.
+
A website, a photograph, a stupid little idea, an experiment. Most of the interesting things I've made started with absolutely no reason to make them.
27.07.26
Why does everything need to be useful?
+
Some things should exist simply because they are interesting. Not every project needs a business model, a KPI or a pitch deck.
19.07.26
Fast iteration beats perfectionism.
+
Shipping a rough concept and refining it in public reveals more feedback than polishing an unreleased project in isolation for six months.
11.07.26
Physical books in a digital age.
+
Tangible pages force focus. When there are no notifications, tabs, or battery indicators, reading becomes a quiet spatial experience again.
02.07.26
The craft of micro-details.
+
Subtle micro-interactions, springy transitions, and thoughtful default values are what elevate functional interfaces into memorable experiences.
21.06.26
Solitude vs. isolation.
+
Solitude is choosing quiet to recalibrate and think clearly. Isolation is being cut off from others. Learning to enjoy quiet space is essential.
14.06.26
The joy of simple web tools.
+
Single-purpose web utilities that do one job exceptionally well without requiring an account or subscription are pure internet gold.
05.06.26
Design constraint breeds clarity.
+
Limiting your palette to two colors or using one typography scale forces you to solve structure through hierarchy and spacing rather than decoration.
28.05.26
Building a personal digital house.
+
Renting profile space on social media platforms will never match owning a personal website where you set every rule, style, and structure.
